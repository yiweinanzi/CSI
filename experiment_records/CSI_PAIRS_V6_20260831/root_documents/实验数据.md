## 3. 预注册主 factorial 统计

主 estimand 是：`equal city x k x base-map-cluster x seed x positive-budget-draw macro utility`。主预算只使用预注册的 `k=0` 和 `k=8`。分层 bootstrap 共 10,000 次，传播 base-map cluster、训练 seed 和正预算标签抽样三层不确定性。

| Arm | Primary macro utility |
|---|---:|
| Endpoint | -2.6686361650 |
| Alignment-only | -2.6828391901 |
| Response-only | **-2.5385957759** |
| Full | -2.6657030121 |

Utility 越高越好。该口径下 Response-only 最好，Full 没有胜过 Response-only。

| Estimand | Estimate | Hierarchical 95% CI | Familywise 95% CI | 判定 |

## 7. 可直接放入论文的结果表述

### 中文

在两个未见目标城市、每城 41 个独立 base-map cluster、3 个训练随机种子的预注册 2 x 2 四臂实验中，Full 模型未显示出优于两个单分支的联合收益。对预注册主预算 `k in {0, 8}`，Full 相对 Alignment-only 的宏效用差为 `0.0171`（分层 bootstrap 95% CI `[-0.1117, 0.1534]`），相对 Response-only 为 `-0.1271`（95% CI `[-0.2780, 0.0171]`）。交互效应为 `-0.1129`（95% CI `[-0.3622, 0.1214]`），未达到预注册的 `+0.02` 最低实际效应。Full 相对 Endpoint 的四个城市-预算检验仅 1/4 通过，因此跨城定位门 G5 失败。结果表明，在当前固定数据、模型和训练配方下，联合监督没有获得可验证的协同收益，Response-only 的主宏效用反而最高。

### English

In the preregistered 2 x 2 factorial experiment with three training seeds and 41 independent base-map clusters in each of two unseen target cities, the Full model did not demonstrate a joint benefit over both single-branch models. Across the preregistered primary label budgets (`k in {0, 8}`), the macro-utility difference was `0.0171` for Full versus Alignment-only (hierarchical-bootstrap 95% CI `[-0.1117, 0.1534]`) and `-0.1271` for Full versus Response-only (95% CI `[-0.2780, 0.0171]`). The factorial interaction was `-0.1129` (95% CI `[-0.3622, 0.1214]`) and did not exceed the preregistered minimum effect of `+0.02`. Only one of four city-by-budget Full-versus-Endpoint checks passed, so the cross-city localization gate G5 failed. Under the fixed data, architecture, and training protocol, the joint objective therefore showed no verified synergy; Response-only achieved the highest primary macro utility.

论文中可以如实发表这批数据，但必须以负结果或诊断结果表述，并保留上述置信区间、门控状态和未评估边界。
